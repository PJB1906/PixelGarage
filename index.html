◆ FAQ

Questions, answered.

What people ask before bringing a project to the garage.

What does PixelGarage do?

PixelGarage is a software studio that designs and builds custom iOS & Android apps, websites and backends for companies — end to end, from product design through to launch and ongoing support.

Does PixelGarage build both mobile apps and websites?

Yes. We build native-quality iOS and Android apps for the App Store and Play Store, custom-coded websites, and the secure backends and APIs that power them.

Where is PixelGarage based and who can hire them?

PixelGarage is based in India and works with companies across India and internationally, delivering projects remotely from idea to launch.

Do you use templates or build from scratch?

Every product is designed and coded from scratch — no templates. UX and UI are designed before any code is written, and you receive full ownership of the backend and codebase.

What projects has PixelGarage shipped?

We've shipped multiple live products, including a platform for Mount Valley School and the RoomAdda room-and-stay booking product, with over ₹4.5L in revenue and every release tested by a team of 25 testers.

How do I start a project with PixelGarage?

Email pixelgarage.info@gmail.com with a short description of your app, website or backend idea, and we'll take it from idea to launch.
